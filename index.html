About

Tsunami Warning is an indie rock band from Merced, California, playing all over the Central Valley and the Bay. Jack and Ziggy started the band in 2022, Hans joined in 2024 and Keiran came aboard on bass in 2025.

Their 2025 album High Tides and 2026 EP By The Sea were both recorded in pieces: drums and rhythm guitar live in a UC Merced practice room, then lead guitar and bass at home. By The Sea features synth and keyboard from ambient artist Jellous. The band has opened for Green Jelly and is now writing singles and a second album.
